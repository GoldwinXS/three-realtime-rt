Kubelka-Munk scattering
Pixels vs. the analytic model. A pigment of known K and S is rendered, read back, and divided by a white Lambert patch under the same directional light — which cancels exposure exactly and leaves the two-flux reflectance R.
Phase 1 is a slab staircase, 10→160 mm: R must climb monotonically toward R∞ = 1/(a+b) and match the reference at every step. Phase 2 is a sphere, probed from centre to rim: the thickness is not authored anywhere, it is whatever the geometry gives the ray, and the chord collapses toward zero at the rim — the degenerate end of the maths.
